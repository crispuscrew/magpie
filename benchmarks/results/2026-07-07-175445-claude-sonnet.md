lines/deps/entities are the magpie cost metric; a floor task passes only if safety survived the prompt. Negative % = cheaper than baseline. Each cell is the median of its reps; the summed row adds those medians across tasks, then compares the totals. A task with no baseline for a metric has no percentage and drops out of the median row, which is why deps reads n/a: a new dependency is always a zero baseline.
